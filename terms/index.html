2. Description of the Service

Peere provides an intelligent, on-device buffer that stages transient media files (such as screenshots, screen recordings, and temporary chat media) for a customizable retention period (default 3 days). Peere allows users to review, whitelist to a permanent Safe Vault, or permanently delete expired clutter via native operating system deletion dialogs.

4. Safe Vault & User Deletion Responsibilities

Peere is designed to empower you with full control over your media storage:

  • Explicit User Action: Peere never deletes photos or videos automatically in the background. Every deletion requires your active initiation and confirmation through the native operating system prompt.
  • Safe Vault: Any photo or video you mark with the Star ("Keep Forever") icon is saved to your Safe Vault and will never be targeted for deletion.
  • Irreversible Deletions: Once an item is deleted and permanently cleared from your device's system trash / "Recently Deleted" album, it cannot be recovered by Peere. You are responsible for ensuring that items you choose to purge are not needed.
